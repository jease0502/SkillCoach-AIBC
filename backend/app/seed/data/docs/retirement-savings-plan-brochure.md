# Retirement Savings Plan Brochure

The Retirement Savings Plan is a variable, investment-linked insurance plan. Premiums are invested in a choice of managed funds, and the plan value moves with the performance of those funds.

## Investment returns

The plan's balanced fund has returned a historical average of 5.2% a year over the last ten years.

Past performance is not indicative of future results. Returns are not guaranteed, and the value of the plan can go down as well as up. The customer may get back less than the premiums paid.

## Access to money

Partial withdrawals are allowed after the first policy year. Withdrawals in the first five policy years incur a surrender charge of up to 5% of the amount withdrawn.

Because of the surrender charge and market risk, the plan is designed for money the customer does not expect to need for at least five years. Customers who may need cash for near-term costs, such as medical bills, should keep that money in a liquid account.

## Free-look period

Every new policy has a 15-day free-look period. If the customer cancels within 15 days of receiving the policy documents, all premiums are refunded.

## Death benefit

The plan pays the higher of the plan value or 101% of total premiums paid if the insured person dies.
