# Sales Playbook: Handling Objections

This playbook describes how Acme account executives handle customer objections. It applies to every call and demo.

## The four steps: Acknowledge, Clarify, Respond, Confirm

1. Acknowledge: show that you heard the concern before answering it. Do not argue or dismiss it.
2. Clarify: ask an open question to find the real concern behind the objection. Price objections often hide a different worry, such as adoption, risk or timing.
3. Respond with value: answer the real concern with specific evidence from the product sheet, such as the free pilot, adoption data or certifications.
4. Confirm: check that the answer resolved the concern, then propose a concrete next step with a date.

## Do not pitch before you understand

Acknowledge and ask before you pitch. Claims such as "we are the best on the market" without evidence lower the customer's trust.

## Discovery questions

Good discovery questions are open and specific, for example: "What happened with the tool you used before?", "Who on your team would use this every day?", "What would success look like after three months?"

## Closing

Every call should end with an agreed next step, such as a pilot kickoff with named participants and a date.
