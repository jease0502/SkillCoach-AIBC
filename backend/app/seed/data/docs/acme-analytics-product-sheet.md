# Acme Analytics Product Sheet

Acme Analytics is a self-service business analytics platform for operations and finance teams. It connects to existing data sources and turns them into dashboards that non-technical staff can use every day.

## Plans and pricing

The Starter plan costs USD 19 per user per month and includes up to 5 dashboards and email support.

The Pro plan costs USD 49 per user per month, billed annually. Pro includes unlimited dashboards, 40+ data connectors, role-based access control and priority support.

The Enterprise plan is quoted individually and adds single sign-on, a dedicated customer success manager and custom data retention.

Volume pricing: teams of 100 users or more receive 10% off the Pro list price. Sales representatives may not offer any discount beyond the published volume pricing without written approval from the sales director.

## Free pilot

Every Pro plan starts with a 30-day free pilot for up to 20 users. No credit card is required, and the pilot workspace converts to a paid plan only if the customer chooses to continue.

## Onboarding and adoption

Every Pro customer gets a guided onboarding program: two live training sessions for team leads, ready-made dashboard templates for finance, logistics and sales, and a weekly adoption report that shows which teams are active.

Customers on the guided onboarding program reach an average of 78% weekly active users after 90 days.

## Security and reliability

Acme Analytics is SOC 2 Type II certified. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).

The Pro and Enterprise plans include a 99.9% uptime SLA, with service credits if monthly uptime falls below that level.

## Contract terms

Annual contracts can be cancelled with 60 days of notice before renewal. Customers can export all dashboards and data at any time.
