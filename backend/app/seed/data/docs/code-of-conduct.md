# Code of Conduct for Financial Advisers

These rules apply to every conversation an adviser has with a customer about investment-linked insurance products. Breaches are reported to the compliance team.

## CC-01 No guaranteed returns

CC-01: Never promise or imply guaranteed returns or a guaranteed principal on investment-linked products.

Prohibited examples: "this plan guarantees you 8% a year", "your principal is guaranteed", "you can't lose money", "it's risk-free".

## CC-02 Risk disclosure

CC-02: Always disclose that the value of investments can go down as well as up.

When quoting historical returns, also state that past performance is not indicative of future results.

## CC-03 No pressure selling

CC-03: Do not pressure the customer to decide in the same meeting.

Prohibited examples: "you need to sign today", "the offer ends tonight", "this price is only for today".

## CC-04 Suitability

CC-04: Ask about the customer's needs, time horizon and need for cash before recommending a product.

Do not recommend a product the customer may need to cash out within five years without explaining the surrender charge.

## CC-05 Free-look period

CC-05: Tell the customer about the 15-day free-look period before the application is signed.
