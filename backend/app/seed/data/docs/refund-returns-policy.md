# Refund and Returns Policy

This policy applies to all products sold in our stores and online. Support agents must follow it in every customer conversation.

## RR-01 Refund window

RR-01: Cash refunds are only available within 30 days of purchase with a receipt.

Agents must not promise or issue a cash refund for purchases older than 30 days.

## RR-02 Outside the refund window

RR-02: Outside 30 days, store credit may be offered at manager discretion.

For defective products outside 30 days, the agent can request store credit or a manufacturer warranty repair (1-year warranty on electronics).

## RR-03 Escalation

RR-03: Escalate to a supervisor if the customer requests it or after two failed resolution attempts.

A supervisor callback is offered the same business day.

## RR-04 Time frames

RR-04: Always give the customer a time frame for the next step.

Store credit requests are approved within 2 business days. Warranty repairs take 7 to 10 business days.
