# Support Tone Guide

How we speak to customers, especially when they are upset.

## Acknowledge emotion first

Acknowledge the customer's emotion before you explain policy. For example: "I'm sorry this happened, that's really frustrating."

Never tell a customer to calm down, and never say it is not your fault or not your problem.

## State what you can do

Focus on what you can do, not what you cannot. Offer clear options and let the customer choose.

## Give a time frame

Always give a time frame for the next step, for example "You'll hear from my supervisor today" or "Store credit is approved within 2 business days."

## Listen for the story

Upset customers often have a personal reason behind the request, such as a gift or an important event. Asking a gentle question can turn the conversation around.
