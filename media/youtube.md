## Title

SkillCoach: AI role-play coach with lifelike customers, built with Gemini on Google Cloud | AI Builder Cup 2026

## Description

New hires in insurance, banking, sales and support usually learn on real customers, and one wrong promise like "this plan guarantees you 8% a year" can turn into a conduct failure. SkillCoach lets them practise the conversation before it counts.

How it works:
- Upload your manuals and policies. Gemini drafts realistic customer personas and scenarios that cite the source passages, and a trainer approves them.
- Practise on a video call with a lifelike customer: fictional people generated with Gemini image generation and animated with Veo, with natural Cloud Text-to-Speech voices.
- The Compliance Guard checks every message before it is sent, quotes the risky phrase and names the rule.
- A coach gives a one-line hint after every reply, grounded in the company playbook.
- Live call mode: turn on your camera (with background blur or a virtual background), talk hands-free, and get on-device expression and eye-contact tracking plus delivery tips from Gemini.
- The report quotes your own words for every score; one serious violation fails the session. Managers see who is ready to go live.

Built on Google Cloud: Gemini 4 and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite on Vertex AI, Veo 3.1, Cloud Text-to-Speech, Cloud Run, Firestore, Firebase Hosting and Firebase Authentication.
