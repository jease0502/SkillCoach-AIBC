SkillCoach demo video: voiceover script (en-US)
Voice: en-US-Chirp3-HD-Charon, speaking rate 1. One paragraph per cue, in order.

[c01_title]
New hires learn on real customers. Role-play needs senior staff nobody can spare, and one wrong promise can cost a company dearly.

[c02_landing]
SkillCoach turns a company's own knowledge into realistic practice conversations, powered by Gemini 4 on Google Cloud.

[c03_knowledge]
An admin uploads product brochures and policies.

[c04_generate]
Gemini 4 reads those documents and proposes realistic customer types.

[c05_sources]
Every objection and hidden need links back to the passage it came from.

[c06_approve]
A trainer checks the sources and approves. Only approved customers are used for practice.

[c06b_scenario]
Scenarios are built the same way. The goal, the compliance rules and the scoring rubric each point to a source, so a trainer can check every rule before it is used.

[c07_open]
Now a new insurance advisor meets Mr. Tan, a cautious retiree, as a 3D customer who reacts to every answer. His first question is a trap.

[c08_guard]
Here's the costly mistake: promising a guaranteed return. Before it reaches the customer, the Compliance Guard stops it, quotes the words, and names the rule.

[c09_send_anyway]
Send it anyway, and trust drops. The coach suggests a way back.

[c10_pressure]
Pressure selling is flagged inline too.

[c11_honest]
An honest answer works better. The customer only states facts from the company's documents, with sources on every reply.

[c12_pipeline]
Each turn runs a pipeline of Gemini agents: compliance judge, customer, grounding check, trust model, and coach.

[c13_end]
Now, the report.

[c14_gate]
One forbidden promise fails the session, however good the rest was. The overall score cannot go above fifty-nine.

[c15_evidence]
Every score quotes the trainee's own words, verified against the transcript. No evidence, no high score.

[c15b_violations]
Each violation names the rule and jumps to the exact turn. Strengths and next steps turn the grade into a coaching plan.

[c16_dashboard]
Managers see aggregates, never transcripts: skill gaps, broken rules, and who is ready to go live. Sample team data is labeled.

[c17_arch]
Under the hood, it's all Google Cloud. Firebase Hosting serves the app, and the web and API run on Cloud Run. Firestore stores documents, personas, sessions and reports. Seven Gemini agent roles run on Vertex AI: Gemini 4 Argon for the customer, builder and report, Flash-Lite for the fast checks.

[c17b_future]
The future of work: a tireless practice partner for every new hire, built from the company's own knowledge.

[c18_end]
SkillCoach. Practice the conversation before it counts.
